# Tuesday review (§1.4, §7)

core has computed the numbers. You interpret them.

## The four things (§7.2)

1. **Result** — won or lost, by how much.
2. **Manager efficiency** — actual starting points ÷ the best lineup available
   in hindsight. This is the only number that measures *the agent*, separated
   from luck. A loss at 98% efficiency is a good week; a win at 70% is a warning.
3. **Calibration** — projected vs actual, by position and archetype. Look for a
   *direction*, not a magnitude.
4. **The league** — every other roster, who is buying, who is selling.

## The rule that matters most (§7.3)

**Distinguish a bad decision from a bad outcome.** Starting the 14-point
projection over the 9-point one was correct even when it scored 3. In a
high-variance game, grading outcomes teaches the wrong lesson.

**Only propose moving a prior on a repeated, directional miss.** One loud week
is noise. A rule that changes every Tuesday is not a rule.
